# 1.24m — formation-first Starting XI display

**Status:** Chromium layout regression passed; development candidate only. The published Pages build remains unchanged.

## User-facing issue

At a 1364px-wide desktop viewport the old tactics page gave the football pitch a 651px-wide column beside the squad panel. Starting XI cards were 104px wide, with 54px portraits; multiple position cards overlapped.

## Change

- Give the tactics pitch the full content width, with the matchday squad and substitutes moved underneath. Team instruction and substitution tools remain available.
- Make on-pitch player cards 156px wide, portraits 86px, and increase readable name/role text and control targets.
- Evenly redistribute visual depth bands for whatever formation is selected. Pitch height scales with the number of depth lines, preventing goalkeeper/defender/midfield/forward card collisions. Player assignment, position, role, stars/attributes and save mechanics are untouched.
- At widths under 1200px retain readable player cards and let the board pan horizontally inside its card; keep the rest of the page within the viewport.
- Package CSS and a narrowly anchored pitch-display change through a verified, non-deploying ZIP builder. Do not alter the released ZIP.
